Youth Empowerment & Development

Empowering the next generation by formulating and implementing policies, programmes, and projects that inspire growth, leadership, and active participation in nation-building.

Our Strategic Mandates

description

Policy Formulation

Formulate, implement, monitor, and evaluate youth development policies, programmes, and projects for sustainable development.

diversity_3

Mainstreaming

Establish mechanisms to integrate youth development into national policies and programmes, ensuring inclusivity across all sectors.

leaderboard

Leadership Participation

Promote active youth participation in leadership and decision-making processes to shape a better, more representative future.

public

Exchange Programmes

Facilitate opportunities for youth to engage in exchange programmes locally, regionally, and internationally for cross-cultural growth.

hub

Coordination

Coordinate youth development initiatives and programmes across public and private sectors for cohesive and sustainable impact.

lightbulb

Opportunities

Collaborate with stakeholders to create diverse opportunities for youth development, skill acquisition, and socioeconomic empowerment.

volunteer_activism

Peace Building

Strengthen youth participation in peace-building efforts to foster harmony, tolerance, and lasting societal cohesion.

handshake

Collaboration

Work with national, regional, and international agencies to enhance youth development programmes through shared expertise.

groups_2

Policy Mobilisation

Mobilise youth to actively participate in ministry and youth-related programmes for holistic personal and national growth.

assured_workload

Regional Conventions

Domesticate regional conventions such as the African Youth Charter and the SADC Declaration for youth empowerment.
