Human Resources

Building and sustaining a capable, motivated, and professional workforce to drive the Ministry's mandate through people-centred HR strategies and staff development.

Our Strategic Mandates

person_search

Recruitment & Selection

Manage transparent and merit-based recruitment processes to attract and retain the best talent for the Ministry's mission and objectives.

school

Staff Development

Design and implement training programmes, workshops, and capacity-building initiatives that empower staff to grow professionally.

leaderboard

Performance Management

Implement effective performance appraisal systems to align staff contributions with the Ministry's strategic goals and service delivery standards.

favorite

Employee Welfare

Promote a healthy and supportive work environment through employee wellness programmes, counselling, and occupational health initiatives.

handshake

Labour Relations

Foster harmonious employer-employee relations through fair dispute resolution mechanisms and compliance with labour laws and regulations.

policy

HR Policy & Compliance

Develop, review, and enforce HR policies and procedures that ensure compliance with the Public Service Act and other relevant legislation.

payments

Payroll Administration

Ensure accurate and timely management of staff remuneration, allowances, and benefits in line with government pay structures.

trending_up

Succession Planning

Identify and groom future leaders within the Ministry to ensure continuity of service delivery and institutional knowledge retention.

folder_shared

Records Management

Maintain comprehensive and secure personnel records systems that support informed decision-making and organizational accountability.
